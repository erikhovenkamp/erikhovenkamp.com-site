01 / About

Biography

Research at the intersection of law, economics, and technological change.

Erik Hovenkamp is Professor of Law at Cornell Law School. His scholarship focuses on antitrust and competition policy, intellectual property and innovation, law and economics, and industrial organization.

He joined Cornell in 2024 after serving on the faculty of the USC Gould School of Law. Earlier, he was a postdoctoral fellow at Harvard Law School and a visiting fellow at Yale Law School's Information Society Project.

His work has appeared in leading law reviews and peer-reviewed economics journals. He holds a Ph.D. in Economics and a J.D. from Northwestern University.
